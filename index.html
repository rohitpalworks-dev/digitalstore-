Good to know

Terms & conditions

This is a demonstration website. Product descriptions and prices are sample content. The cart is for demonstration only: checkout is unavailable, no order is placed, no payment is taken, and no digital product is delivered.

Do not rely on this demo as an offer to sell or as a completed purchase. Before using this site for real sales, the store owner should provide accurate product and pricing information, delivery details, refund rules, and terms that apply to their business.

Privacy

Local accounts are stored in a SQLite database on the computer running this site. Passwords are stored as salted scrypt hashes, not plain text. Signing in uses an HttpOnly session cookie.

This local site does not send verification emails or newsletter email addresses. Submitting the sample newsletter form only displays a message in your browser.

Your demo cart is saved in this browser's local storage so it can remain between visits. It is not sent to a store server by this page. You can clear it through your browser's site-data settings.

Keep the server on this computer only. This local setup is not configured for public hosting or production customer accounts.

Contact

No support email or contact channel is configured for this demo. Add your real business contact details here before publishing a live store.
